Wingspan Opportunity Foundation

Expanding access. Extending opportunity.

Our Mission

Wingspan Opportunity Foundation expands access to Montessori education, from infancy through high school, for children in Texas whose families face financial barriers.

Our Vision

Montessori education open to every child, not only those who can afford it, so that a generation of deeply educated, emotionally intelligent, and responsible young people can build a more humane and connected world.

We envision a future in which a child's access to Montessori education depends on who they are, not on what their family can afford. Children who grow up in authentic Montessori environments develop a depth of understanding, a sense of personal responsibility, and an emotional intelligence that shape the people they become. We want every child, whether gifted, struggling, or somewhere in between, to be truly seen and supported in growing into their full potential.

What We Do

Wingspan provides scholarships that help Texas families afford authentic Montessori education, with awards scaled to each family's need. By opening that path to more children and to the educators who guide them, we believe we can help raise a generation of independent, connected, and compassionate people.

Our Values

  • Every Child Seen. We see each child for exactly who they are, and we believe in the potential already within them.
  • Integrity. We act with honesty, fairness, and transparency in every decision and with every dollar entrusted to us.
  • Responsibility. We trust children with real responsibility, and we hold ourselves to the same standard in how we serve families, schools, and donors.
  • Connection. We build relationships grounded in care and emotional intelligence, because learning happens in relationship.
  • Depth. We invest only in authentic, high-quality Montessori education, because access to a watered-down version is not the life-changing experience we are working toward.

Get Involved

Whether you are a family seeking support, a school interested in partnership, or a donor who believes every child deserves this education, we would love to hear from you. Contact us at hello@wingspanopportunity.org.
