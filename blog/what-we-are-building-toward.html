What We Are Building Toward

October 27, 2026

I have spent most of this blog showing you the hard math of childcare, the margins, the regulations, and the cost of doing this work well, so today I want to write about the other side of the ledger, which is what all of that math is actually for.

We have huge dreams for The Nest over the next five to ten years. We would love to see our fifty acres built into a farm school one day, with a campus that runs all the way through high school. We have started a nonprofit, the Wingspan Opportunity Foundation, to help students gain access to Montessori education, not just at our school but all over Texas. On the ten year list is something this region genuinely lacks: our own AMI Montessori training center right here in the Austin area, so the next generation of guides does not have to leave Texas to be trained at the highest standard. We have already built a learning record app, and we plan to grow it into full childcare management software. And through Classroom Comeback, we go into other childcare centers and help them clean, purge, reorganize, and reset their systems, sometimes all the way to a grand reopening, because rising tides should lift every school, not just ours.

Alongside all of that, the philanthropic work continues to grow. We have partnered with United Way in Williamson County, joined the Success By 6 coalition, and volunteered with local nonprofits, and that community layer is not a side project. It is part of what we are.

What excites me most about all of it is impact. My entire life, I just wanted to do good. I went into mental health originally because I fell in love with understanding people and thought I could make a difference for people who were suffering. And I have always loved and understood children, even when I was one myself. I believe helping children is what will ultimately change the world and make it better, more connected, more loving, more sustainable, because I think people are starving for connection and most do not even realize it.

Now, the CFO part of me has to say the unsexy part. None of these dreams happen without stability at each benchmark first. You cannot build the top of the house when the foundation is sticks and toothpicks, and we have already learned what happens when you try to expand before the base is solid. So right now, we are doing the unglamorous work on purpose: holding steady, training, reorganizing, and investing in AMI training for our staff, because between AMI, our Cognia accreditation, and Texas Rising Star, the quality track we are on matters more to me than the speed. Honestly, The Nest is better today than it has ever been, and that is because we chose the foundation over the headline.

And underneath every system, process, and every dream is the same simple hope. I want every child we touch to remember their days at The Nest with love and fondness. We genuinely care about each child, and we see each child, and I have truly never watched anyone connect with every single child the way Shavonda does, because that is her heart and her purpose. We want to build each child up until they can see and reach their full potential, not just in a career someday, but in life. We want to see them happy, connected, purposeful, encouraged, and educated.

That is what we are building toward. The margins are thin, the rules are many, and the dreams are still bigger than both.
